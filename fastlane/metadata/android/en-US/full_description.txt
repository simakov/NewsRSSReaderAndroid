A news reader for the public RSS feeds of Lenta.ru, a Russian news site. This is an unofficial app: it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Lenta.ru.

It only reads the news — no accounts, no comments, no recommendations.

<b>Features</b>

* Three feeds: top stories, latest, and everything
* A drawer with all 15 sections, from sport to economics
* Full article view: paragraphs, subheadings, photos, pull quotes, info boxes and author bylines
* Read articles are dimmed in the list automatically
* Bookmarks save an article's text and photos for offline reading
* Photos open full screen, with pinch-zoom and save-to-gallery
* Pull past the end of an article to open the next one

<b>Privacy</b>

The app collects nothing about you. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no ads and no trackers — it uses the network only to fetch the feed, the article text and its images.

Free software under the GPLv3; the source is open.
