The app is now called Lenta Reader, and the ".RU" is gone from its wordmark: this is an unofficial reader of the Lenta.ru public RSS feeds, not affiliated with its newsroom.

A new "About" row at the bottom of the category drawer shows the installed version and opens the project on GitHub.

All analytics have been removed — the app no longer reports anything about you. The source is now under the GPLv3.
