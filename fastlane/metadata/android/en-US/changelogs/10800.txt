Photos are now sent through the system share sheet: pass them to a messenger or save them to Files. The original file goes out, not a recompressed copy, and the app no longer needs access to your device's storage.

In the fallback reading mode (when an article opens as the site's own page) the app no longer loads third-party counters and ads, and links to other sites open in your browser.

Stories from other sites, such as moslenta.ru, no longer appear in the feeds.
